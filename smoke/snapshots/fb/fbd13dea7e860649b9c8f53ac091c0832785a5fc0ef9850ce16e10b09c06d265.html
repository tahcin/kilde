NRP Investering

We invest in companies with a clear strategic fit

NRP Investering is an accelerator deploying fresh capital in new projects and long-term investment strategies with substantial balance sheet capacity.

NRP Investering is our investment vehicle, allowing us to invest in NRP-related projects and funds, in addition to external companies with a clear strategic fit. We invest in companies where we can add value, and scale the business. Our primary focus is to build and grow our asset management practice.
